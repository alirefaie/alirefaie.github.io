Measuring How Palestinians Frame Their Rights: A 1,000-Response Survey

Role: survey instrument design, digital data collection setup, and the raw data pipeline.

~1,000
responses collected
XLSForm
instrument standard
2026
fieldwork

The problem

Measuring public attitudes on how rights are framed and perceived in Palestine requires more than writing questions. Opinion data at this scale is only as good as the instrument and the collection pipeline behind it, so the work started before the first response arrived.

The data

Roughly one thousand responses, fielded in June 2026. The questionnaire was authored as an XLSForm, the standard that powers digital collection tools such as ODK and KoboToolbox, so every question, choice list, constraint, and skip rule lives in one auditable definition file.

Methodology

  • Authored the survey instrument in XLSForm: question logic, constraints, and skip patterns defined up front rather than patched in the field.
  • Set up the digital collection pipeline so responses arrive structured and coded, not as free text to reconcile later.
  • Cleaned the raw export of about one thousand rows: completeness checks, coding validation against the form definition, and duplicate review.

Results

A clean, analysis-ready dataset where every variable maps back to a defined question in the form specification, ready for descriptive and inferential analysis of public attitudes.

Commissioning details are anonymized. No respondent-level data appears on this site.
