Does Digital Transformation Pay Off? Evidence from Palestinian Businesses

Role: end-to-end analysis, from survey design through inferential testing.

Survey
primary data collection
SPSS
analysis environment
Hypothesis
testing at stated significance

The problem

Businesses everywhere are told to digitize, but advice is cheap and evidence is not. This project asked an empirical question about the Palestinian market: does digital transformation actually change business performance, and which dimensions of it matter?

The data

Primary survey data collected from businesses, structured around the study's constructs. The survey instrument was reviewed against the research questions before fieldwork, so the analysis could genuinely answer them rather than approximate them.

Methodology

  • Designed the survey instrument around the research questions and hypotheses.
  • Cleaned the response data and built analysis-ready variables, including composite scores for the study's constructs.
  • Profiled the sample with descriptive analysis, then tested each hypothesis about digital transformation and business performance at a stated significance level.
  • Documented every test choice so the results can be reviewed and reproduced.

Results

Each hypothesis got a clear verdict, supported or not, with the evidence behind the call. The result is a quantitative account of how digital transformation relates to business performance in the Palestinian context, built on data collected for exactly that question.

Commissioning and identifying details are withheld for confidentiality. No respondent-level data appears on this site.
