About

Analyst, researcher, and statistics educator.

I am a data analyst and biostatistician pursuing an MSc in Applied Statistics and Data Science at Birzeit University (expected 2027). My work sits at the intersection of survey research and applied statistics: taking research teams and organizations from a raw questionnaire export to results they can defend in a journal submission, a board meeting, or a public report.

A typical project covers instrument design, data collection setup, cleaning and validation, the statistical analysis itself, and clear reporting of what the data does and does not say. I also publish educational content that explains biostatistics methods in plain language, because the right test matters more than a complicated one.

At a glance

  • Qualification: MSc candidate, Applied Statistics & Data Science, Birzeit University (expected 2027)
  • Tools: SPSS, R, Python, Stata, XLSForm/ODK
  • Methods: hypothesis testing, regression, non-parametric tests, time series, survey statistics
  • Sectors: health research, education, business and policy surveys
